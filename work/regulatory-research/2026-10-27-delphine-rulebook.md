## 1. Summary

California AB 2030 is now law (Chapter 720, Statutes of 2026) and takes effect January 1, 2027, per a trade association. From that date, online sellers of supplements "labeled, marketed, or otherwise represented" for weight loss or muscle building must verify buyers against a third-party database or ship with an 18+ adult signature and ID. The factors a court may weigh include green tea extract and green coffee bean extract, so both Delphine's copy and any botanical caffeine source in the formula matter. The question the owner most needs counsel to answer has also sharpened: in 2021 FDA told a seller of a non-swallowed caffeine product that it was "not intended for ingestion", so not a dietary supplement, and an unapproved drug. FDA has since told NBC that caffeine pouches are regulated "as either dietary supplements or conventional foods, depending on their composition and marketing". No state in the 12 priority markets defines nicotine or tobacco products in a way that, on its face, reaches a nicotine-free caffeine pouch. The bigger near-term blockers are on the channels: TikTok and Meta ban nicotine pouches and "smoking simulants" or products that "simulate smoking".

## Decisions needed (for the coordinator to log in `work/decisions.md`; this agent did not edit it)

1. **Regulatory path (blocks product and label):** FDA's 2021 Eagle Energy letter treated a non-ingested caffeine product as not a supplement and as an unapproved drug, and FDA says pouches are supplements or foods "depending on their composition and marketing". The owner needs counsel's written choice of path before label design or supplier commitment.
2. **Caffeine source in the formula (affects CA, RI and NJ exposure):** green tea and green coffee bean extracts are named intended-use factors in CA AB 2030 (in force 2027-01-01) and the RI and NJ bills. Owner to decide, with counsel and product-development, whether to specify caffeine anhydrous only.
3. **Online age verification standard (affects the store):** AB 2030 requires a third-party database check or an 18+ ID-checked signature for covered products. The owner needs to set the age (still treated as 21+) and choose the verification method, with counsel deciding whether AB 2030-grade checks apply nationally.
