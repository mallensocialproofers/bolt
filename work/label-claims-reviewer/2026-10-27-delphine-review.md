## Rulebook gaps and UNVERIFIED items that limit this review

- **FDA category is unresolved** (supplement vs conventional food). Label panel (Supplement Facts vs Nutrition Facts), the AE address duty, and even whether label wording such as "Do not swallow" is safe all depend on counsel's answer. The 2021 Eagle Energy letter is the key risk (rulebook 2a). Every label line below is therefore provisional on that.
- **No FDA, state or bill text names caffeine pouches** (rulebook section 3). "Not found" is not "no law": 30 non-priority states were only grouped-searched; CA AB 2030 chaptered text, IL 720 ILCS 675/1, MI MCL 722.644, TX "tobacco substitute", DE HS 1 final text were not read.
- **Platform text not read or UNVERIFIED:** Meta job-title targeting, TikTok Shop, Reddit, Amazon, PayPal, Square, UPS, FedEx, Google's unapproved-substances list. Meta, TikTok and Google rules on "nicotine pouches" and "smoking simulants" are High confidence but whether they reach a nicotine-free caffeine pouch is open.
- **Pregnancy/lactation numbers:** rulebook now has ACOG "<200 mg/day" at Medium (PubMed abstract; ACOG page and reaffirmation date not read) and CDC "300 mg or less" at High (page updated 2026-08-25). PD still calls these "UNVERIFIED secondary numbers". That is stale: PD should cite the rulebook sources, but ACOG remains Medium, and no number should go on a label without counsel.
- **400 mg figure source:** FDA consumer page, High (page current 2024-08-28), wording "an amount not generally associated with negative effects" for most adults. CLAUDE.md's first mention is a trade site (tobaccoinsider.com), not FDA. PD correctly cites FDA. The words "healthy adults" come from H.R. 2511 / NY S9158 bill text and CLAUDE.md, not from the FDA page as the rulebook quotes it; quote the FDA wording exactly if used.
- **Pending label rules to design around:** NY S9158 (caffeine over 10 mg per serving, 400 mg advisory, website "high caffeine" designation for added caffeine) and H.R. 2511. Both are bills, not law (status beyond referral UNVERIFIED for H.R. 2511).
- **FTC 16 CFR 255 and 465** are "carried forward" in the rulebook (High, not re-read this pass). I did not open them either.
- **Delivered caffeine dose per pouch is unknown** (rulebook 2d; PD section 5). Label states content, not absorbed dose; no onset or absorption wording anywhere.
