# Emmeline brand plan (draft, 2026-10-27)

Status: draft for the owner, to be reviewed by `label-claims-reviewer` and counsel before anything is public. It assumes the SelfNamed face-value operating model in `business-plan.md` (private label, stock formulas, dropship, no inventory). Nothing here is a clearance; the name has an open collision (EMMELINE SKIN). All copy below is claim-disciplined by design and still needs review.

## 1. Brand essence
**Positioning (two sentences):** Emmeline is a quiet, well-made eye and face ritual for adult women who value a slow, considered moment in a busy day. It is sold simply, with plain language, a clear subscription and no promises about what it will do to your skin.

**Why this works with the rules.** The house rules for this line ban drug, health, fatigue, alertness and sleep claims, any wording that implies the viewer looks tired or has a condition, appearance-attacking before/afters, and nurse-as-expert endorsement. The brand therefore sells **ritual, restraint, texture, craft and honesty**, not results. The luxury cue comes from design, packaging, tone and service, not from a claim of an exclusive or clinical formula (the formulas are SelfNamed catalogue stock; see section 9).

**Brand pillars**
1. **Ritual, not repair.** The product is a small daily act, shown in the hands, the bathroom shelf, the quiet minute.
2. **Plain language.** Short sentences, the full ingredient list as printed on the label, the price and the subscription terms stated up front.
3. **Considered design.** Restrained packaging, a good box, a card that explains how to use it.
4. **Generous service.** Easy skip, pause and cancel; honest shipping times.

## 2. Audience (adults only; sourced where possible, else labelled hypothesis)
- **Who:** women about 35 to 60, with a focus on women who work overnight shifts as nurses. About 87% to 89% of RNs are women (BLS via USAFacts; NCSBN 2024 via secondary summaries); NCSBN 2024 median age 50; BLS median RN pay $97,550 (SRC-U). A Nurse.org 2026 survey says about a quarter of nurses say pay barely covers essentials, and 55% of Americans plan to cut subscriptions (SRC-U): price clarity and easy cancellation matter.
- **What we do not know:** what these women re-buy, how often, what they would pay, or how they talk about it. No quotes from the target women exist in the research. Everything below is a hypothesis to test with a short survey and manual reading of community threads.
- **How copy refers to them:** it does not name nurses, night shifts or tiredness (implied-claim and Meta personal-attribute risk, per the earlier label review). They are reached by where the brand shows up (communities, creators, search), not by labelling them.

## 4. Voice
**Tone:** calm, warm, precise, a little dry. Short sentences. No hype, no exclamation marks, no urgency tricks, no slang, no puns on age or tiredness.
**Principles:** say what it is; say how to use it; say what it costs and when it renews; never say what it will do.

**Word list (for `label-claims-reviewer` and counsel to confirm)**
| Safer words | Avoid entirely | Confirm with counsel first |
|---|---|---|
| ritual, routine, texture, lightweight, cream, mist, pump, 15 ml, 100 ml, evening, morning, simple, considered, gentle (only if substantiated), the full ingredient list, price, subscription, skip, pause | tired, puffy, dark circles, wrinkles, fine lines, firm, lift, repair, regenerate, collagen, youthful, anti-age, age-defying, brighten, dark spots, calming, soothing a condition, microbiome, barrier repair, clinical, dermatologist-tested (unless true and documented), doctor, nurse, night shift, exhausted | moisturizing, hydrating, smoothing the look of skin, "suitable for sensitive skin", "fragrance-free" (only after the ingredient list), "cruelty-free", "vegan", "natural", "clean", "luxury" |

## 6. Visual identity (suggestions for a designer; describe, never copy another brand)
- **Mood:** warm, quiet, tactile, daylight. Think paper, linen, ceramic, brass.
- **Palette (proposed hex values):** Ivory `#F4EFE8` (primary background), Warm Stone `#D9CFC3`, Clay `#B88A78`, Plum `#4E3443`, Ink `#2A2428`, Brass `#A98B5B` (accent, small use).
- **Typography:** a high-contrast serif for the wordmark and headlines, a clean humanist sans for body and the subscription terms. License fonts properly; do not imitate a brand's custom typeface.
- **Logo:** a wordmark "Emmeline" in the serif, with a small descriptor line "Eye & Face Care"; a simple monogram "E" for the favicon and stamps.
- **Packaging (SelfNamed offers custom labels and boxes and a Design Studio, per its pages; confirm what is included and the cost):** stock bottle and pump in matte white; the luxury cue comes from the label, the box (matte ivory, plum print, a brass-coloured detail if offered), tissue wrap and an insert card with the routine and subscription terms. The label carries the legally required items (identity statement, net contents, full ingredient list, responsible-person name and address, warnings), reviewed by counsel.
- **Photography direction:** daylight, still life first (jar, mist, linen, marble, a hand), adult women visibly over 40 with real skin texture and no airbrushing, close crops on hands and shoulders rather than faces, no hospital, scrubs or night imagery, no before and after, no implied results.

## 8. Website and customer experience
**Pages:** Home; Eye Cream; Face Mist; The Pair; The Ritual (how to use); Subscription terms; Ingredients (once verified); FAQ; Shipping and returns; About (the owner's real story, honest); Contact.
**Home page order:** hero (a still-life image and one line), the two products, The Pair, three plain promises (plain ingredient list, easy cancel, honest shipping), the ritual, FAQ.
**Subscription UX (ROSCA and state auto-renewal laws; a lawyer reviews):** price, cadence and cancellation terms visible next to the add-to-cart button; express consent at checkout; an account portal with skip, pause and cancel; a reminder email 7 days before renewal; online cancellation with no phone call; no annual prepay at launch (chargeback and reserve exposure).
**Shipping:** advertise a range ("ships in 3 to 7 business days", confirm with SelfNamed), free over $60, $5.95 below. State the real range on the page; the FTC mail-order rule defaults to 30 days when none is stated.
**Returns:** set a clear policy after SelfNamed answers its refund and defect questions.
**Unboxing:** box, tissue, the product, a card with the routine and subscription terms, no promotional claims.
**Email flows:** welcome (3 emails); order and shipping; pre-renewal reminder; skip/pause confirmation; win-back after a cancellation (no pressure); review request (do not condition or reward based on rating; FTC Reviews Rule).

## 9. Honest-luxury guardrails
- The formulas are SelfNamed stock; do not claim exclusive, proprietary, clinical or "formulated by" language unless true. Value comes from design, service and curation.
- A $48 eye cream on a roughly $9 catalogue cost invites "overpriced" reviews if the experience does not match; test price cells ($38 to $54) and watch reviews.
- No nurse-as-expert endorsements; nurse creators, if used, must disclose any connection, and brand and creator are both liable (16 CFR 255). Prefer adult micro-creators talking about routine, not results.
- No claims, no before/afters, no comparisons to named brands, no "doctor recommended".
