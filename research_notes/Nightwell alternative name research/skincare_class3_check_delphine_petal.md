# DELPHINE and PETAL in cosmetics classes (Class 3 and 44), 2026-10-27

Context: the owner asked whether Delphine or Petal could be a skincare / undereye brand. Earlier screens covered classes 5, 29, 30, 31, 32, 34, 43 (caffeine pouches) and deliberately excluded Class 3. Source: USPTO search backend (undocumented; recent filings may be missing). Not a clearance.

Web search: no Delphine eye-cream brand found; Delphine Breyne LLC holds DELPHINE PARIS / DELPHINE B. PARIS skin-care filings (Justia owner page).
Verdict: DELPHINE is crowded and directly on point in Class 3 for skincare. The earlier "clean" finding applies only to caffeine pouch classes.
