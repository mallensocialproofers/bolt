# .com-first name search (2026-10-27)

Owner constraints: needs a bare .com, easy to sell, somewhat feminine but marketable. Adult only: soft and elegant, no cute, candy, teen, alcohol or dessert cues; no wellness, sleep or alertness words.

Method: Verisign RDAP (HTTP 404 = not registered in .com; confirm at a registrar, premium or reserved names can still 404). Not a trademark clearance.

## Results
- 145 pretty real words and names (odette, velour, marlowe, pearl, saffron, ...): 0 free bare .com.
- 119 hand-picked coined names and night/lune/nuit compounds: only luneeve, luneroze, noyeve, nuitbelle, rosenuit free (all awkward).
- 1,470 automatic prefix+suffix blends: 282 free but mostly gibberish.
- 6,000 pronounceable CVCVC+ending strings: 3,864 free but random strings read as mush.
- 2,128 pretty-word + second-word combos: 1,012 free. Pattern "[pretty word] + society / club / tin / tins / supply / house / social / pouch(es)" is mostly free, e.g. odettesociety, odetteclub, marlowesociety, mirabelsociety, delphinesociety, colettesociety, noellesociety, isadorasociety, rosalindclub, esmesociety, perlaclub, eloisesociety, margauxclub, camillesociety, juliettesociety, anoukclub.
- Free single-word .com among earlier shortlists: nachtra.com only.
