NOT a legal clearance. Nothing here says "Bolt" is clear or safe. Take to a trademark attorney. Access note: USPTO Trademark Search/TSDR was not accessed directly (only third-party mirrors, several returned HTTP 403 to me); Google Trends, Ahrefs/Similarweb (need auth), Reddit, Instagram/TikTok handle checks, state registries and WIPO were not accessible. ## 1. Name conflicts (BOLT in food, beverage, supplement, nicotine, vape, caffeine)

### Takeaway
There is at least one direct, live-looking conflict: boltpouches.com sells "BOLT" caffeine energy pouches (50-200 mg), registered 2026-06-24, so same name, same product format. Gatorade Bolt24 (caffeinated variant, Class 32), PROBAR Bolt (caffeinated chews) and several BOLT/BOLT-formative Class 34 vape records also exist. Status and ownership of the USPTO records could not be confirmed from primary data.

## 5. Voice, tone and youth-appeal risks

### Takeaway
Category voice is punchy and slangy ("bold", "tax", "lip pillow" on TikTok); schools are banning caffeine pouches because they resemble nicotine pouches. A calm, adult, label-forward voice and strict age controls are the defensible lane.

### Cited Findings
- ABC7 Chicago (2026-05-25): Onsted Middle School (MI) said pouches "closely resemble nicotine pouches in appearance and use"; Holton schools (KS) banned them; pediatric ED physician says caffeine absorbs faster than from coffee; AAP does not recommend caffeine for children; TikTok tags include "upper decky" (9,000+ posts), "lip pillow" (10,000+), "caffeinepouch" (about 2,200). — [ABC7 Chicago](https://abc7chicago.com/post/what-are-caffeine-pouches-schools-banning/19169168/)
- No federal minimum age for caffeine pouch purchase (per a seller's blog, not an FDA doc); many retailers self-set 18+; H.R. 2511 (Sarah Katz Caffeine Safety Act) reintroduced 2025-03-31 would require caffeine labeling above 10 mg. — [Takeroon age article](https://www.takeroon.com/blog/how-old-to-buy-caffeine-pouches) (2026-08-02; commercial source, verify against congress.gov)
- Wip's homepage voice includes "Flavors as bold as you are" and candy-like flavors (Watermelon, Blue Raspberry) with no age statement. — [Wip](https://www.wip.com)

### Inferences
- Avoid: candy/fruit-flavor cues and cartoon art, slang like "lip pillow"/"decky", influencer/gaming/school-adjacent channels, nicotine-lookalike tin design, and any 18+ framing; use 21+ age gate, plain mg labeling, "adults only" statements.
- Do not describe health, focus or performance effects; stick to facts (mg per pouch, ingredients, flavor, count).
