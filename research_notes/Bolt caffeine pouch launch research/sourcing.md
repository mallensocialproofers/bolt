## 5. Red flags

### Takeaway
Main risks: unverifiable factories, regulatory classification uncertainty in the US, and thin certifications.

### Cited Findings
- Sourcify's red-flag list (audit refusal, no recall insurance, vague ingredient sourcing, below-market pricing, no certs, NDA refusal) and claim that "61% of Alibaba-listed 'manufacturers' are trading companies" (unsourced) — [Sourcify China](https://www.sourcifychina.com/caffeine-pouch-manufacturer/)
- US regulatory status of caffeine pouches unsettled: a Feb 2026 testing-firm analysis argues non-swallowed pouches may fall outside the DSHEA dietary-supplement definition and could be addressed under FDA's 2018 highly-concentrated-caffeine guidance (could not verify against FDA text); FDA's 2026 agenda includes draft caffeine-labeling guidance for foods/beverages; no federal age limit; Delaware and New York proposed high-caffeine labeling rules — summarized from search result, [BSCG blog](https://www.bscg.org/blogs/single/energy-pouches-safety-ingredients-and-compliance-risk) (note: the page I fetched was about India/anti-doping and did NOT contain this analysis, so the DSHEA/2018-guidance claim is UNVERIFIED), [Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/caffeine-labeling-fda-guidance-celsius-alani-nu/824355/) (not opened)
- Denmark: authority commissioned a safety assessment and issued a draft order targeting caffeinated sachets (Feb 12, 2026) — [NutraIngredients](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/02/12/authorities-play-catch-up-on-caffeine-energy-pouch-regulation)

## 6. Draft RFQ email (DO NOT SEND)

Subject: RFQ - Private-label oral caffeine pouches, 25 mg, US launch

Hello [Name],

We are launching a private-label, nicotine-free oral caffeine pouch for the US market and are evaluating contract manufacturers. Please send a quote and the information below.

Product: 25 mg caffeine per pouch (target label claim; please state your fill tolerance and the lowest/highest dose you hold reliably), tobacco- and nicotine-free, [flavors: X, Y, Z - or your stock library], [N] pouches per tin (open to your standard count).

1. Pricing per tin (and per 1,000 pouches) at 10k, 25k, 50k and 100k tins; one-time tooling/setup/artwork fees; tin and label costs listed separately.
2. MOQ per SKU and per flavor; first-run vs repeat-run lead time; payment terms and deposit.
3. Facility: address, FDA food facility registration number, cGMP status (21 CFR 111 or 117), third-party audits/certifications (NSF, SQF, etc.) with certificate copies.
4. Quality: caffeine assay method (e.g., HPLC), COA per lot, microbial/heavy-metal/foreign-matter testing, content-uniformity data, stability/shelf-life data.
5. Materials: pouch fleece material and supplier, caffeine source (synthetic/natural, USP/FCC), sweeteners, flavor suppliers, allergen statement.
6. Packaging: tin options, child-resistant closures and test certification, label/printing capabilities.
7. Samples: cost and timing for formulation and flavor samples.
8. Insurance (product liability/recall), recall procedure, and willingness to sign an NDA and a quality agreement.

Please also share 2 references from comparable brands. Happy to schedule a call.

Thanks,
[Name / Company / Phone]

## 7. Five questions for a supplier call
1. Which regulatory framework do you manufacture caffeine pouches under (dietary supplement cGMP vs. conventional food), and can you show your FDA registration, last audit report, and any FDA 483s or warning letters?
2. How do you guarantee a 25 mg dose: what is your fill-weight tolerance, assay method (HPLC), content-uniformity result, and what happens to a lot that fails?
3. What are your MOQ, lead time, and price breaks per SKU, and which are one-time (setup, tooling, artwork) vs recurring?
4. What pouch material, caffeine source and flavors/sweeteners do you use, who supplies them, and can you provide COAs and allergen documents?
5. Which tin and child-resistant options do you offer or integrate, who owns the tin inventory, and do you provide samples and a recall/insurance policy?
