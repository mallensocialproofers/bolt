# Channels and Payments: Policy Landscape for Selling Caffeine Pouches Online (US)

Checked 2026-10-27. Framing: every item is a question for counsel or the processor, not a compliance conclusion. Coverage is partial: many official pages returned errors, truncated text, or were summarized by the fetch tool rather than quoted in full. Quotes below are fragments returned by the fetch tool; re-verify against the live page before relying on them.

## Ad platforms: are caffeine pouches / supplements / pouch look-alikes allowed?

### Takeaway
No platform policy found names caffeine pouches. Meta explicitly bans nicotine pouches and "products that simulate smoking" even without nicotine (vapes), which is the closest look-alike language found. Google, TikTok, YouTube, X, Reddit, Snapchat and Pinterest were not verified.

### Cited Findings
- Meta Tobacco and Related Products: "Ads must not promote the sale or use of tobacco or nicotine products and related paraphernalia." Nicotine pouches are listed among prohibited items. — [Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/tobacco-related-products/) (checked 2026-10-27)
- Same page: ads must not promote "products that simulate smoking, such as vapes, including products that don't contain tobacco or nicotine (e.g., vitamin-based vapes, wellness vapes)". The page does not use the word "lookalike", and no pouch-specific non-nicotine language appeared. — [Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/tobacco-related-products/)
- Meta Drugs and Pharmaceuticals page: the fetch returned no dietary supplement or caffeine section. The only supplement wording returned: ads can't "Encourage the consumption of illicit, recreational, or other potentially unsafe drugs, products or supplements." Meta's supplement rules may sit on a different page. — [Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/drugs-pharmaceuticals/)
- Google Ads Dangerous products page (answer 6014299), as returned: "Ads for substances that alter mental state for the purpose of recreation or otherwise induce" are not allowed. The fetch tool did not return the unapproved-substances or supplements sections. — [Google](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6014299)
- Google tobacco/nicotine policy: UNVERIFIED. Answer 9475042 returned 404, and answer 176031 returned an unrelated healthcare page.
- Google "Unapproved pharmaceuticals and supplements" policy (answer 6008942): UNVERIFIED. The fetched text did not contain it.
- TikTok Ads policy on drugs, tobacco and weapons: UNVERIFIED (page returned an error). TikTok Shop: not fetched. YouTube, X, Reddit, Snapchat, Pinterest: not fetched (UNVERIFIED).
- Third-party agency guidance (a lead only, not policy): nicotine-free caffeine pouches are not auto-blocked as tobacco on Meta, have no dedicated category, and fall under general unsafe-substances and health review at reviewer discretion. — [adsuploader summary via search](https://adsuploader.com/blog/meta-ad-guidelines) (search snippet only, page not fetched)
- Brand evidence: a Shopify podcast description of Cyclone Pods (a zero-nicotine vape company with a planned "Focus Pouch" caffeine/nootropic pouch) says "Every ad platform banned him" and "Paid ads are off the table." The text concerns the vape business generally, not caffeine pouch ads specifically. — [Unofficial Shopify Podcast](https://ethercycle.com/blogs/unofficial-shopify-podcast/the-restricted-goods-playbook-no-ads-no-stripe-no-usps)
- Search found no public report of a caffeine pouch brand's ad rejection or account ban on Meta or Google. — [search 2026-10-27]

### Inferences
- Meta's "simulate smoking... including products that don't contain tobacco or nicotine" language shows Meta treats the look and use of a product, not only its contents, as relevant. Whether that extends to oral pouches is a question for Meta/counsel.
- Because nicotine pouches are banned, creatives that resemble a Zyn-style tin or pouch may draw automated or human flags even if the product is caffeine only.

### Gaps
- Exact Google, TikTok, YouTube, X, Reddit, Snapchat and Pinterest wording not retrieved. Questions for counsel: does each platform treat oral pouches as nicotine by appearance, and is caffeine a restricted supplement ingredient?
- No first-hand brand accounts of ad rejections.

## Payments

### Takeaway
Shopify Payments defers to the underlying processor's prohibited list. Stripe's list was only partially retrieved. High-risk processors market to nicotine pouch sellers, and no caffeine-specific processor evidence was found.

### Cited Findings
- Shopify Payments terms: prohibited and restricted business lists "are provided by each Payment Processor in your Designated Country" (see the Payment Processor List); lists are "meant to be representative but not exhaustive." — [Shopify Payments terms](https://www.shopify.com/legal/terms-payments-us) (2026-10-27)
- Stripe restricted businesses (as returned): "Tobacco products, including e-cigarettes, cigars, and e-liquid sold in accordance with applicable law"; US merchants "may only sell products authorized by the Food and Drug Administration"; "Herbal and vitamin e-cigarettes and cigarettes" listed; prohibited: "Pseudo-pharmaceuticals or nutraceuticals that are not safe or make harmful claims." Caffeine and nicotine were not named. — [Stripe](https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses)
- Tower Payments (a high-risk processor) launched a service for online nicotine pouch sellers, Jan 6, 2026: "many processors will block a nicotine seller's account under acceptable use rules." No fees or reserves stated in the retrieved text. — [Digital Transactions](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/tower-payments-launches-new-solution-for-online-nicotine-pouch-sellers)
- Cyclone Pods podcast listing mentions "The Shopify Payments shutoff" (the teaser also says "Stripe cut him off"; the sources are inconsistent) for a zero-nicotine vape business. — [Unofficial Shopify Podcast](https://ethercycle.com/blogs/unofficial-shopify-podcast/the-restricted-goods-playbook-no-ads-no-stripe-no-usps)
- A January 2026 article says Shopify Payments guidelines treat CBD, cannabis and tobacco as restricted or heavily scrutinized (search snippet). — [eCommerce Fastlane](https://ecommercefastlane.com/shopify-payments-risk-profiles/)
- PayPal AUP: UNVERIFIED (truncated). Square, Authorize.net: not fetched. Underwriting requirements, reserves and fee ranges: no sourced numbers found; none given here.

### Inferences
- Whether processors read a caffeine pouch as "nicotine-pouch-adjacent" is the key underwriting question to ask directly, with product photos in hand.

### Gaps
- Fee/reserve ranges, named high-risk processors that accept caffeine pouches, Square/Authorize.net/PayPal wording.

## Email / SMS

### Takeaway
Klaviyo does not address supplements or nicotine pouches by name; SMS rules are stricter.

### Cited Findings
- Klaviyo AUP: for US/Canada short codes and toll-free numbers, content about "Illegal substances" is prohibited, with examples including "Kratom, vaping/e-cigarettes, THC, and related paraphernalia products"; also "Sex, hate, alcohol, firearms, and tobacco (SHAFT)." The policy "does not separately address nicotine products" or supplements. — [Klaviyo AUP](https://www.klaviyo.com/legal/acceptable-use-policy) (2026-10-27)
- Mailchimp: not fetched (UNVERIFIED).

### Inferences
- A pouch product could be treated by an SMS carrier as SHAFT-adjacent. Question for Klaviyo/carrier: is a nicotine-free pouch acceptable for 10DLC/toll-free registration?

### Gaps
- Mailchimp, app stores, carrier (CTIA) text.

## Shipping carriers

### Takeaway
No carrier rule on caffeine pouches found. Nicotine pouch carrier treatment is unverified.

### Cited Findings
- General background only: the PACT Act makes cigarettes and certain tobacco products nonmailable through USPS and imposes delivery-seller requirements; UPS banned vape shipments from April 5, 2021. None of the results addressed nicotine or caffeine pouches. — [County of Merced](https://www.countyofmerced.com/4295/Prevent-All-Cigarette-Trafficking-PACT-A); [Vaping360](https://vaping360.com/de/vape-news/108064/ups-will-end-home-delivery-of-vaping-products/)
- Cyclone Pods listing claims "how UPS and FedEx said yes and USPS said no" under the PACT Act for its (vape) products; this relates to its nicotine-free vape, not confirmed for pouches. — [Unofficial Shopify Podcast](https://ethercycle.com/blogs/unofficial-shopify-podcast/the-restricted-goods-playbook-no-ads-no-stripe-no-usps)
- UPS tobacco page returned Access Denied (UNVERIFIED). USPS, FedEx, DHL: not fetched.

### Gaps
- Primary carrier text. Question: does any carrier consider a pouch with no nicotine "tobacco-like" at acceptance?
