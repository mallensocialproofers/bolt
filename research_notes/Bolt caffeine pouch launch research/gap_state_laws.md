## 1. Verification of named leads

### Takeaway
No state bill or law found that names "caffeine pouch", "caffeinated pouch" or "oral pouch product" for caffeine. The nearest are Delaware HB 394 (signage only, passed House) and the weight-loss/muscle-building age laws (NY enacted, CA signed 9/28/2026), none of which mention caffeine in the sources read. Exposure turns on definitions and on Bolt's marketing claims, which I could not confirm from statute text.

One source says original version would have barred under-18 sales; no source explicitly confirms the ban was removed. UNVERIFIED (secondary; legis.delaware.gov and legiplex returned errors/403) — [DE House Dems 6/12/26](https://housedems.delaware.gov/2026/06/12/house-passes-neal-bill-to-better-regulate-highly-caffeinated-beverages/); [NatLawReview](https://natlawreview.com/article/delawares-house-representatives-passes-caffeine-warning-signage-bill); [DE House GOP 5/2/26](https://housegop.delaware.gov/2026/05/02/caffeine-regulation-bills-delaware/)

### Inferences
- If the weight-loss/muscle-building statutes work as summarized, they key on marketing claims; a pouch marketed only as a 25 mg caffeine product would not obviously be in scope, but counsel should confirm, and "performance/fat-burn" copy should be avoided.
- Direction of travel is signage and under-18 limits; an adults-only age gate is consistent with every bill found. Online sellers are expressly in scope in NY/CA, so a verification flow matters if a claim brings Bolt in.

## 3. Nicotine/oral-pouch definitions: in or out for non-nicotine pouches

### Takeaway
Every definition seen is nicotine-based, so caffeine-only pouches are not covered on their face, but only summaries were read and the 12 priority states were not each checked.

### Cited Findings
- Oregon SB 1571 (signed March 2026, effective 6/5/2026) expands "tobacco products" to oral nicotine pouches, lozenges, gum and other nicotine products, natural or synthetic; statutory text not read, so whether nicotine is a required element is unconfirmed — [Oregon OHA 6/3/2026](https://www.oregon.gov/oha/erd/pages/oregon-law-expands-tobacco-products-definition-to-prevent-underage-sales-youth-nicotine-addiction-06.03.2026.aspx)
- NY S9954 (pending, status unconfirmed) would add oral nicotine pouches to flavor ban; NY taxes tobacco-free nicotine products at 75% wholesale from 9/1/2026 (secondary) — [NY Senate S9954](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S9954); [Fox5 NY](https://www.fox5ny.com/news/nicotine-vape-laws-across-northeast-what-you-need-know-2026)
- CA: flavored-tobacco law reported to reach flavored nicotine pouches (UCSF comment) — [UCSF comment 5/2026](https://profglantz.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ucsf-comment-on-utl-regs_051726.pdf). NJ flavor ban covers vapes, synthetic nicotine; North Bergen local flavor ban 1/1/2026 (Fox5). Hawaii SB2384 (2026) is a proposal on oral nicotine pouches — [HI SB2384](https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/sessions/session2026/Bills/SB2384_.PDF).
- Prop 65 notice found against nicotine pouches (nicotine), not caffeine — [CA AG notice 2024-01664](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/prop65/notices/2024-01664.pdf)

### Inferences
- Retail/practical risk remains from look-alike packaging and shared retailer policies, not from statute text found.
