# Who buys caffeine/energy oral pouches in the US, and which niches existing brands target (checked 2026-10-27)

Method note: WebSearch returns summarised snippets; several primary pages (Wip/Ultra press releases, Black Rifle, Fast Company original) returned 403/405 to WebFetch, so some facts rest on search-engine summaries of the primary page (flagged "via search summary"). Competitor-authored/brand-authored sources are marked UNVERIFIED (self-reported). No Google Trends data was accessible. No Fortune/AP/NBC pieces were found by search. Nothing below is a survey of pouch buyers by occupation: that data was not found.

## 1. Which buyer segments do existing brands target in their own marketing?

### Takeaway
Brands' own copy skews to broad "energy without the can" positioning plus a few concrete occupational hooks: Roon (shift/police/night workers, students, travelers), Wip (athletes/gym and hands-on trades like construction), Grinds (ex-chewing-tobacco/baseball, military/veteran via Black Rifle), Ultra (high performers/celebrity athletes, "ZYN user" angle). No brand found owns truckers or gamers in its own pages. All of these brand sources are UNVERIFIED as evidence of who actually buys.

### Cited Findings
- Roon "Night Shift" page headline "Caffeine for the night shift"; copy refers to nights, handover, twelve-hour days; links to study, travel-day/jet-lag pages; no named occupation on that page. Page is marketing; carries FDA disclaimer; makes performance claims (not to be copied by us). - Roon "Patrol" page positioned for police shifts (80 mg caffeine, 15/tin) — [Roon patrol page](https://www.takeroon.com/pouches/patrol-cup-goes-cold) (via search summary; UNVERIFIED, competitor)

### Inferences
- Occupational hooks used publicly: shift/night/police (Roon), construction/trades and gym (Wip), military/veteran/tactical (Grinds x BRCC), military/nurses/first responders (Ultra discount). Truckers are most associated with the gas-station channel (Walmart Fuel Centers, QuikTrip/Sheetz), not named in any brand's own copy that I could read.
- Because Roon, Ultra and Wip already touch shift workers, first responders and trades, a pure "shift worker" claim is crowded; a narrower vertical with a named community may be less contested.

### Gaps
- Not read directly: Nectr, Wild Pouches, NZE, Bucked Up, Neutonic, Doseology and Roon main-site copy (not found in search; Firecrawl unavailable). Wip, Ultra, Grinds first-party pages returned 403.
- No evidence found of any brand sponsoring gamers, golfers, hunters, or truckers specifically.

## 2. What public sources say about who buys

### Takeaway
No credible published survey breaking caffeine-pouch buyers down by age/gender/occupation was found. Published evidence is: brand press releases, trade-press placement info, and commentary that points to young, active, male-leaning, convenience-store and online shoppers. Most claims are brand-authored.

### Cited Findings
- Wip cites its own study of 10,000+ people aged 21-40, "nearly 50%" purchase intent (sentence truncated in the snippet) — [The Grocer (UK)](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/analysis-and-features/caffeine-pouches-health-hazard-or-next-big-energy-trend/714665.article) (via search summary; UNVERIFIED, brand-run research)
- Research & Markets projection: US caffeine pouch market $71.3M this year rising to $107.2M by 2032 — same Grocer article (via search summary; market-research projection, not verified)
- Commentator J. Schall: US nicotine-free mouth-pouch market could exceed $1B in a decade only if c-stores support the energy format; target buyers include ZYN users seeking cognitive use, athletes, discreet-product seekers — [NutraIngredients 2026-02-05](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/02/05/are-energy-pouches-the-next-big-thing-in-active-nutrition) (opinion; no demographic data in article)
- YPulse (2026-06-11): caffeine pouches "a trend, especially among U.S. teens"; no numbers; sourcing of "caffeine as alcohol substitute" is Dazed — [YPulse](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2026/06/11/gen-z-is-helping-create-a-caffeine-boom/); [Dazed](https://dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/70422/1/how-caffeine-became-gen-z-drug-of-choice-sober-curious-wellness-drink-pouches). WARNING: teen-appeal narrative is a regulatory and PR risk; consistent with the owner's no-minors rule.
- Adjacent: nicotine-pouch use in the US (2022-23 TUS-CPS) is ~0.77% of men vs ~0.09% of women, ~0.65% of 18-24 and ~0.66% of 25-44; nearly absent among tobacco-naive adults — [JAMA Network Open](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2838608) (via search summary; this is nicotine pouches, not caffeine; and "former nicotine users" is an excluded angle)
- Tobacco/age-verification: USA Today (via blog reprint) says brands claim adult-only but pouches have no energy-drink-style age limits — [blog reprint](https://homeword.com/?p=42857) (UNVERIFIED, secondary)
- Reddit: archived r/ thread on USA Today "Young people are obsessed with caffeine pouches", 16 comments, mostly sceptical — [Reddit snapshot, 2026-02-11](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1r1y02i/snapshots/2026-02-11T18%3A50%3A24.64759Z). A Roon blog claims to summarise 13 nootropic-pouch Reddit threads — [Roon blog](https://www.takeroon.com/blog/nootropic-pouches-reddit-what-users-say) (UNVERIFIED, competitor)
- College students: 92% consumed caffeine in the past year (all forms, not pouches) — [LSU repository](https://repository.lsu.edu/psychology_pubs/982) (via search summary; age of study not verified)

### Inferences
- Likely core buyer is a male 21-40 who already uses pouches/dip/energy drinks, but the available proof is brand-sourced; flag as hypothesis.

### Gaps
- No Fortune, AP, NBC, NACS, Tobacco Insider, Beverage Daily articles found by search; no Circana/NielsenIQ pouch data; no age/gender/occupation breakdown of caffeine-pouch buyers; Wip Walmart/Ultra releases only via summaries.
