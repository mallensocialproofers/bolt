# Channels and acquisition for a 25 mg adults-only caffeine pouch brand (US), by niche reachability

Checked 2026-10-27. Tools: WebSearch/WebFetch only (Firecrawl out of credits). Search results are model-summarised, so anything not quoted from a primary page is UNVERIFIED. Nothing here is a legal conclusion; items are questions for counsel. About 15 tool calls were used, so coverage of several sub-questions is thin (see Gaps). Prior notes (channels_and_payments.md, gap_channels_policies.md) were read and are not repeated except where they bear on reachability.

## Paid channels: do caffeine/energy pouch brands run Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, X, podcast, Twitch ads, and what do they cost?

### Takeaway
No platform wording was found that names caffeine pouches, and no public report of a caffeine-pouch ad ban was found, but also no first-hand evidence that Nectr/Roon/Wip/Mojo/Ultra/Grinds run paid ads (Meta Ad Library was not retrievable). Treat paid social as "unverified, possibly allowed, high look-alike risk", and plan on organic, creator, search-content and retail as the dependable channels. Published CPM/CAC numbers exist only from agency blogs and are wide and contradictory.

### Cited Findings
- Meta Ad Library: could not be retrieved via search; a search for Nectr/Roon/Grinds returned no Ad Library data. Only a third-party aggregator (Particl) shows a Grinds Instagram post from July 2025 (organic post/aggregator, not Meta records). Ad activity of Nectr, Roon, Wip, Mojo, Ultra: UNVERIFIED/not found. — [Particl Grinds](https://www.particl.com/grinds-coffee-pouches/late-night-pick-me-up-1049795-ef6143f8) (checked 2026-10-27); Ad Library itself: [facebook.com/ads/library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library) (not queried, requires manual search by owner).
- Roon (a competitor, brand-published, dated Aug 4, 2026) argues a zero-nicotine product is eligible to run standard paid ads on Meta, YouTube and Twitch subject to general policy, but quotes no platform language supporting that; it quotes Meta ("can't run ads that promote the sale or use of tobacco or nicotine products or related paraphernalia"), YouTube ("content that promotes a product that contains drugs, nicotine, or a controlled substance") and Twitch advertiser-friendly guidelines ("Drugs, Intoxication, or Excessive Tobacco Use"). UNVERIFIED (competitor, self-serving). — [Roon blog](https://www.takeroon.com/blog/nicotine-pouch-streamer-sponsorship-ad-restrictions) (checked 2026-10-27)

### Inferences
- If Meta/Google ads do run, the realistic planning CAC range from these vendor sources is roughly $40-$120 per new customer, but nothing is specific to caffeine pouches, and a low-AOV pouch product (see subscription section) would sit at the high end of payback risk. This is an inference from non-specific sources.
- A look-alike creative risk (Zyn-style tins) is the main paid-social uncertainty; using visibly caffeine/coffee-coded packaging in niche-friendly creative would lower, not remove, that risk.

### Gaps
- Google, TikTok, YouTube, X, Reddit, podcast and Twitch ad policies for caffeine pouches: not retrieved. Meta Ad Library activity per brand: not retrieved (owner should search it manually). Podcast and Twitch sponsorship rates: none found. No published CPC/CPM specific to energy-drink DTC found.

## Organic, creator, affiliate, SEO, email/SMS, communities, sponsorships

### Takeaway
Creator and affiliate routes are what competitors visibly use; creator deal prices were not disclosed anywhere found. Competitors' own comparison-listicle SEO (Roon) is visible, but no performance data. Email/SMS carries SHAFT-adjacent risk (prior notes).

### Cited Findings
- A small caffeine pouch brand (Sznpouches) lists no open affiliate campaigns on Grin and invites custom creator pitches, with content "during work, study, gaming, training, travel, or night shifts". Roon/Nectr affiliate programs: not found. — [Grin profile](https://app.grin.ai/brands/sznpouches.com) (checked 2026-10-27, via search summary)
- Commission benchmarks from adjacent caffeine/coffee programs (not pouches): Incredibrew Coffee 20% with 30-day cookie; mCaffeine (Indian skincare) 10% base up to 15%; Incapto 60-day cookie via CJ. — [Awin Incredibrew](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/120617); [Otto mCaffeine](https://joinotto.com/en-in/influencer-programs/mcaffeine); [Incapto](https://incapto.com/en/pages/programa-de-afiliados) (search summaries; UNVERIFIED for pouches)
- Roon publishes comparison/"where to buy" content such as "caffeine pouches at Walmart" and "what gas stations sell caffeine pouches", showing the listicle/SEO play. — [Roon Walmart](https://www.takeroon.com/blog/caffeine-pouches-at-walmart); [Roon gas stations](https://www.takeroon.com/blog/what-gas-stations-sell-caffeine-pouches) (titles seen in search; competitor content, claims UNVERIFIED)
- Klaviyo AUP: US short-code/toll-free prohibits "Illegal substances" (kratom, vaping, THC) and SHAFT (incl. tobacco); nicotine and supplements not separately addressed. — [Klaviyo AUP](https://www.klaviyo.com/legal/acceptable-use-policy) (from prior notes)
- Esports, trucking, sports-league, podcast sponsorship rates for caffeine pouches: none found.

### Inferences
- SEO/listicle and creator content are the channels that avoid ad-policy dependency; niches with active search demand ("coffee alternative at work", "night shift energy") are better reached this way, but this is untested.

### Gaps
- Competitor affiliate terms, creator rates, Reddit promotion rules (subreddit-level), esports/trucking/league sponsorship pricing, SEO traffic data.

## Retail and wholesale: c-stores, truck stops, gyms, vape shops, exchanges, airports, grocery, distributors

### Takeaway
Convenience/fuel is the proven brick-and-mortar route for category leaders (Wip, Grinds), but brands there are 100-200 mg, not 25 mg, and how newcomers are listed, slotting fees and margins were not found in credible sources. Age gating at shelf is store discretion, not law.

### Cited Findings
- Wip announced June 15, 2026 that its pouches are in 330+ Walmart Fuel Centers across 17 states (AL, AZ, CA, CO, FL, GA, IL, KY, MI, MO, NM, OK, PA, SC, TX, UT, WV); 100 mg and 200 mg strengths; also sold at Love's, Murphy USA, QuikTrip, Sheetz, Wawa per its release. Fetch of the release returned 403, so this is from search summary of press-release pages. — [Nosh](https://www.nosh.com/pr/2026/06/15/wip-energy-pouches-now-available-in-over-330-walmart-fuel-centers-nationwide); [Fitt Insider](https://insider.fitt.co/press-release/wip-energy-pouches-now-available-in-over-330-walmart-fuel-centers-nationwide/) (checked 2026-10-27). Brand claim; UNVERIFIED independently.
- Wip says it passed 2.5 million units sold in year one (press release, June 3, 2026). — [Nombase](https://www.nombase.com/newswire/2026/06/03/wip-surpasses-25-million-units-sold-in-year-one-proving-consumers-are-ready-for-energy-beyond-the-traditional-formats) (brand claim)
- Roon (competitor) says Grinds is stocked at 17 named chains including Casey's, Love's, Pilot, Buc-ee's, Murphy USA, Maverik, per Grinds' locator; no source found that TA carries caffeine pouches; placement (behind counter or open shelf) not found. UNVERIFIED. — [Roon gas stations](https://www.takeroon.com/blog/what-gas-stations-sell-caffeine-pouches)
- Distributors: Core-Mark's 2020 10-K describes it and McLane as the two national US convenience distributors; thin distributor margins (~1% EBITDA at Core-Mark in 2018 per an investor write-up); Core-Mark's 2012 10-K mentions manufacturer-sponsored sales/marketing programs. No slotting fee, minimum, or new-brand terms found. — [Core-Mark 10-K FY2020](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318084/000131808421000012/core-20201231.htm); [Value Investors Club](https://valueinvestorsclub.com/idea/CORE_MARK_HOLDING_CO_INC/6435928598)
- Retail margins: no energy/pouch-category markup found; only fuel margins (35.7 cents/gal, OPIS Jan 30, 2025) and a 2020 NPS bulletin citing a 10.5% gasoline markup, not relevant to pouches. In-store foodservice gross margins 30%-67% per a NACS-based summary. — [NPS bulletin](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/concessions/upload/2021-Convenience-Store-and-Fuel-Markup-Technical-Bulletin-11-30-2020.pdf); [NACS](https://www.convenience.org/stay-current/conveniencecorner/who-makes-money-selling-gas)
- Age gating: a vaping retailer guide says no federal minimum age for caffeine pouches as of Sept 2026 (Tobacco 21 does not cover nicotine-free products), stores may set own policies. Whether shelved next to nicotine pouches: no evidence found. Canadian industry group's nicotine pouch commitments (behind counter, ID under 25) apply to nicotine only. — [Vaping360](https://vaping360.com/learn/how-old-to-buy-caffeine-pouches/); [CCentral](https://ccentral.ca/all-we-are-asking-fairness-concerning-sale-nicotine-pouches) (retailer/trade sources, UNVERIFIED)
- Nectr requires 18+ online; Mojo says no age restriction but not recommended under 18. — [NBC New York](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/national-international/cross-between-nicotine-pouch-energy-drink-caffeine-pouches/6341787/) (from prior notes)

### Inferences
- Because retailers set their own age policy and shelving may put caffeine pouches near nicotine products, Bolt's adults-only posture would need to be written into retailer agreements (counsel question). Truck-stop and fuel-center chains carry competitors, so that route looks open to the category but not proven for a 25 mg newcomer.

## Subscription/DTC conversion and retention benchmarks

### Takeaway
Vendor blogs put supplement subscription churn around 5-8% monthly with a steep early drop-off; numbers conflict and lack methodology.

### Cited Findings
- Typical DTC supplement subscription churn 5-8% per month (below 5% strong, below 4% top-tier); another Eightx piece says replenishment categories average under 4% monthly (conflict). — [Eightx supplement churn](https://eightx.co/blog/supplement-subscription-churn-rate-benchmark); [Eightx by category](https://www.eightx.co/blog/average-subscription-churn-rate-by-category)
- Supplement cohorts retain 45-62% at month 6 and about 37% at month 12; average lifetime 12.5-20 months; 60-70% of subscribers lost between order 1 and 3 across most categories; supplements have the "leakiest first reorder"; non-subscription repeat purchase ~29%; annual vs monthly plans 28% vs 11% 12-month retention. — [Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/supplement-subscription-churn-rate-benchmark); [Ecommerce Fastlane](https://ecommercefastlane.com/supplement-subscriptions-have-the-leakiest-first-reorder-but-investors-dont-seem-to-mind/); [Ringly](https://www.ringly.io/blog/customer-churn-statistics-2026) (vendor aggregates, UNVERIFIED)

### Gaps

## Niche reachability (ranking inputs)

### Takeaway
Evidence does not directly rank niches; it supports a framework: favour niches reachable through organic search, creators and retail without needing paid social, and that do not require performance language or youth appeal.

### Cited Findings
- Competitors' own copy for use cases ("work, study, gaming, training, travel, night shifts") shows which niches brands try to reach. — [Grin Sznpouches](https://app.grin.ai/brands/sznpouches.com)
- Trucking/fuel-center retail has confirmed competitor presence (Love's, Pilot, Murphy USA). — [Roon gas stations](https://www.takeroon.com/blog/what-gas-stations-sell-caffeine-pouches) (UNVERIFIED)
- Gaming/streaming niche: Twitch advertiser guidelines flag drugs/tobacco content (Roon quote), and the leading cited creator deal is a streamer (LUCY, nicotine). — [Roon blog](https://www.takeroon.com/blog/nicotine-pouch-streamer-sponsorship-ad-restrictions)

### Inferences
- Likely easier: adult occupational/shift-work (trucking, night-shift) via retail and SEO, and adult hobbyist/professional segments via niche creators; but performance/"alertness" claims are banned for Bolt so messaging must be taste/convenience only.
- Likely hardest: gaming/esports and college/student (skew young; adults-only exposure risk; Twitch ad friction), and gym/athletic (implies performance and overlaps with nicotine-pouch look-alike risk). These are inferences, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No quantitative reachability data per niche; audience size and cost per niche not found.
