## Which coined names show the fewest collisions, and how do the 18 candidates screen?

### Takeaway
All 18 exact-match .com domains are already registered (Verisign RDAP, 200 on every one), so none is available as a plain .com. That includes 'get<name>.com' for 6 of them and '<name>co.com' for 5. Web searches surfaced no exact-match live trademark or brand in food, supplement, caffeine or nicotine for most candidates. That is weak evidence: Justia/Trademarkia pages were blocked (HTTP 403) or not indexed by the search tool. Lenvo is rejected outright, because it reads as a near-homophone of Lenovo (a heavily enforced mark).

### Inferences
- Because every exact .com is taken, the realistic route is a modifier domain such as <name>pouches.com (free for all 18), or a ".co"/other-TLD plus an acquisition inquiry; the coined-word advantage on domains did not materialise.
- Names ending in a recognisably pharma-like suffix (-ane, -eo) can sound like drug brands; that conflicts with "no health implication" and should be weighed.
- Kaffo and Mesura registrations expire within ~3 weeks (2026-11-11, 2026-11-20); RDAP shows no "pending delete", so renewal is the likelier outcome.

### Gaps
- No USPTO/WIPO/Trademarkia result could be read: Justia search returned 403, and the search tool surfaced only near-neighbour records. Every "no exact match" is UNVERIFIED and must go to a proper knockout search (classes 5, 29, 30, 32, 34, 35, phonetic variants).
- Spanish/French/German/Portuguese/Hindi/Mandarin/Arabic/Russian meanings were not sourced; the column above is from model knowledge only.
- Domain content/for-sale status and social handles: not checked.
- Per-name searches for '<name> caffeine' and '<name> pouches' were done only for a subset.

## Ranked top 5 (deep-screen limited by tool access; shallow-confirmed items only)

### Takeaway
Doseo, Alvane, Meliro, Tessal, Oravo are the best of a weak field. None is verified clear. Ranking weighs: no exact collision found, free pouches.com/get.com/co.com, fit with calm, adult, dose-forward tone.

### Cited Findings
1. **Doseo** — dose-forward, fits "label-forward and transparent about dose"; no exact mark found; the nearest, DOSED, LLC (food, Classes 29/30), is in an adjacent class — [trademarkdir](https://trademarkdir.com/owner/dosed-llc). Domain: doseo.com registered 2007 to 2032 (long-term holder; unlikely to sell cheaply); getdoseo, doseopouches, doseoco free. Risks: DOSED similarity (Class 30 food), sounds medicinal/pharma, could be read as implying a prescribed dose.
2. **Alvane** — neutral, calm, no meaning found; no exact hit; nearest ALVAN (tools) and ALVANSO (cosmetics pending) are different goods. Domain: alvane.com registered 2017, expires 2027-04-04; other three patterns free. Risks: "-ane" chemistry ending; AVELAINE (supplements, live application July 2026, mirror snippet) is a similar-looking supplement filing; unsourced language checks.
3. **Meliro** — soft, pronounceable; no exact hit; MELIPRO is Class 12. Domain: meliro.com registered 2020, expires 2027-07-05; others free. Risks: Latin "melior" suggests "better" (mild implied claim); not checked in other languages.
4. **Tessal** — crisp, short; nothing found for Tessal + caffeine/pouches/supplement. Domain: tessal.com registered 2016, expires 2027-09-25; others free. Risks: possible resemblance to drug brand "Tessalon" (unverified); sounds like "vessel/tassel".
5. **Oravo** — "ora" hints at oral use (honest for a pouch); no exact hit; neighbours Orava, oBravo are appliances/audio. Domain: oravo.com registered 2003 with delete/renew/transfer/update locks (indicates registry-lock-level holder, likely a company); others free. Risks: close to Orava/oBravo in sound; "oral" cue edges toward nicotine-pouch vocabulary.

### Inferences
- Any of the five needs a full USPTO knockout and attorney review before adoption; Doseo's meaning is the strongest brand fit but also the highest regulatory-tone risk.
- If an exact .com matters, ask the registrants of alvane.com / meliro.com / tessal.com (shorter-held, non-locked) about price; doseo.com and oravo.com look less promising.
