## 2. Deep screen and ranked top 5

### Takeaway
Forenoon is the best of this territory on the evidence gathered: no live mark found, plain adult meaning, no youth or drug connotations found, and modifier domains unregistered. Elevenses has the strongest ritual story but live marks in Class 25 and 43 and a for-sale .com. All five still need a full USPTO knockout search and counsel review.

### Cited Findings and ranking

**1. Forenoon** (FOR-noon; "Forenoon Pouches" / "Forenoon Coffee Co.")
- Trademark: only FORENOON TIMES, Class 21, dead/abandoned 10 Nov 2025 — [Trademarkia](https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?query=forenoon)
- Domain: forenoon.com registered since 2005-03-23, expires 2027-03-23 (RDAP). Web fetch returned empty content, so the use is unknown. forenoonco.com, getforenoon.com, forenoonpouches.com unregistered by RDAP.
- Search: "Forenoon" with coffee/tea/energy/vape/nicotine returned no brand — [WebSearch 2026-10-27](https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?query=forenoon) (search results did not surface a brand; weak evidence).
- Meaning: English word for the morning before noon (dictionary pages seen in search). Foreign-language meanings and slang not checked.
- Risks: dated/archaic, two-"n" spelling and pronunciation guess; forenoon.com owner unknown and could be approached or may be an active brand; "fore-" could suggest golf.

**2. Elevenses** (ih-LEV-en-ziz; "Elevenses Coffee Co.")
- Trademark: live ELEVENSES Cl. 25 (Urban Outfitters) and ELEVENSES FRESH TABLE & BAR Cl. 43 (FHE, LLC); dead Cl. 30 bakery, Cl. 33 spirits, Cl. 43 — [Trademarkia](https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?query=elevenses)
- Domain: elevenses.com registered since 2000, redirects to a GoDaddy for-sale page (price not read). elevensesco.com registered. getelevenses.com, elevensespouches.com unregistered by RDAP.
- Search: Rare Tea Company sells an "Elevenses Gift Set"; an Elevenses café exists in Tampa (Toast ordering) — [Rare Tea Co](https://rareteacompany.com/products/elevenses-gift-set), [Toast](https://www.toasttab.com/local/order/elevensesco). No dedicated packaged coffee/snack brand found.
- Meaning: British mid-morning tea or snack break (Rare Tea page describes it as a mid-morning tea ritual).
- Risks: existing food-service and tea uses (Cl. 30/43 adjacency), obscure to many US adults, hard spelling, "-es" plural reads slightly whimsical.

**3. Pausa** (PAW-sah; "Pausa Pouches")
- Trademark: live PAUSA Cl. 13 firearms; LA PAUSA Cl. 42 bars/restaurants and Cl. 3 Chanel perfume; 8 of 18 results unseen — [Trademarkia](https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?query=pausa)
- Domain: pausa.com, getpausa.com, pausaco.com registered; pausapouches.com unregistered by RDAP.
- Meaning: "pause" in Spanish/Italian/Portuguese (my general knowledge, UNVERIFIED here); a pause fits the calm positioning.
- Risks: weakest domain situation; possible café-use conflicts; "pause" could be read as sleep or stimulant-break messaging, so label wording must stay neutral.

**4. Noonday** ("Noonday Pouches")
- Trademark: NOONDAY live in Cl. 14/18/others (Noonday Holdings, scarves and accessories; a known fashion brand), Cl. 36 (Farallon), Cl. 35/41 (Noonday Ventures); 7 results unseen — [Trademarkia](https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?query=noonday)
- Domain: noonday.com registered since 2002 (web fetch failed); getnoonday, noondaypouches, noondayco unregistered by RDAP.
- Risks: established unrelated brand and multiple live marks; "noonday" has a religious/literary flavor; possible dilution or likelihood-of-confusion arguments in Cl. 35 retail.

**5. Deskside** ("Deskside Pouches")
- Trademark: DESKSIDE pending Cl. 3 hand lotions (A to Z International, as of 6 Jul 2026); DESKSIDE MENTOR live Cl. 41 — [Trademarkia](https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?query=deskside)
- Domain: deskside.com is an active IT-services business (web fetch), not for sale. getdeskside, desksideco, desksidepouches unregistered by RDAP.
- Risks: reads as IT support; narrows the brand to office use; pending Cl. 3 filing from another owner.

### Inferences
- Forenoon and Elevenses best match the "adult, calm, label-forward" brief; neither carries nicotine, youth, slang, or energy cues in anything I read, though I did not check slang sources.
- Because "forenoon" implies a specific time of day, it could be read as a morning-only claim; that is a positioning choice, not a legal finding.
- None of the five reads like a pouch brand or like Nectr/Roon/Zyn-style names.
