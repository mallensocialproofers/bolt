## 1. Dropship, POD and no-MOQ suppliers/platforms (model, shipping, pricing, branding, minimums, origin, complaints, recurring risk)

### Takeaway
Only a few options give both branding and no minimums. Printful has no order minimum and offers inserts and custom packaging (the packaging only if you stock inventory in its warehouse). Spocket and Zendrop sell branded invoicing or private-label tiers by subscription, but I could not confirm real shipping times or packaging terms from primary pages. CJ publishes a 10-per-variant / 100-total minimum only for stocking its overseas warehouses. For a nurse-focused recurring product, the practical no-MOQ branded paths are POD (apparel, mugs, eye masks, if the product fits) or small-batch private label (skincare is the category where "no minimum" is most advertised). Supplements and similar products usually carry much higher minimums.

### Cited Findings
- Spocket plans (monthly billing): Starter $39.99 (25 unique products), Professional $59.99 (250 unique, 25 premium), Empire $99.99, Unicorn $299.99; all list branded invoice; 7-day trial. Page gives no shipping times and no sample policy. — [Spocket pricing](https://www.spocket.co/pricing) (primary, checked 2026-10-27)
- Spocket says it connects to US and EU suppliers; a competitor-authored note says China-origin products on such platforms can still take weeks. — [Spocket pricing](https://www.spocket.co/pricing); [Spocket CJ overview](https://www.spocket.co/blogs/cjdropshipping-overview) (vendor-authored on a competitor, UNVERIFIED)
- Zendrop: free tier plus paid plans; one review lists Pro $49/mo and Plus $79/mo and a usage-based option from $29/mo; plans month-to-month per Zendrop support. Review sources disagree on which tiers include branding. — [Zendrop support plans article](https://support.zendrop.com/en/articles/9981459-understanding-zendrop-pricing-plans-a-complete-guide) (primary, only search snippet read); [Tools for Humans review](https://www.toolsforhumans.ai/ai-tools/zendrop) (UNVERIFIED)
- CJ Dropshipping: US-warehouse delivery claims range from 2-3 days (CJ pricing page per a review) to 3-6 days (CJ blog) to 4-10 days (App Store listing); China warehouse about 7-22 days. Stocking overseas warehouses needs at least 10 pieces per variant and 100 in total. A US warehouse may not hold the exact variant. Complaints: shipping variability, uneven sourcing-agent support, costly express on low-ticket items; one Shopify reviewer says they would not use CJ for branded dropshipping again. Shopify App Store rating about 4.9 (about 2,500 reviews); no verified Trustpilot figure found. — [CJ blog](https://blog.cjdropshipping.cn/detail/Unlock-3-Day-Delivery-with-CJ-s-New-US-Warehouses) (vendor); [Spocket CJ overview](https://www.spocket.co/blogs/cjdropshipping-overview) (UNVERIFIED); [Shopify reviews](https://apps.shopify.com/reviews/2375006); [Capri review thread](https://appnavigator.io/app/cucheng/reviews/2228449) (UNVERIFIED)
- Printful: no order minimum (Shopify listing note); US/Canada Standard shipping 3-4 business days after fulfillment per Printful support; third-party sources cite 2-7 business days production. Offers packing inserts and custom packaging (custom packaging if stocking in its warehouse). — [Printful support, shipping options](https://support.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/41396648644497-What-are-Printful-s-shipping-options) (primary); [Printful on Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/Printful); [ecommerce-platforms](https://ecommerce-platforms.com/articles/how-long-does-printful-take-to-ship) (UNVERIFIED)
- Printify: timing, price and packaging depend on the chosen print provider; branding options include custom neck labels, inserts, gift messages. — [Printify blog](https://printify.com/blog/t-shirt-dropshipping/) (vendor, UNVERIFIED)
- Faire (buying side): brands set their own first-order and lower reorder minimums, and Faire lets buyers filter for no or under-$100 minimums. Faire Source has a $100 minimum and is invite-only (planned expansion 2026). Brand free-shipping thresholds of $300/$500 come from a coupon site only. — [Faire help](https://www.faire.com/en/support/articles/29090131049371) (primary per search snippet); [Faire retailer guide](https://blog.faire.com/buying/retailer-guide-to-buying-on-faire/); [dealhack](https://dealhack.com/coupons/faire) (UNVERIFIED)
- Amazon MCF: ships in unbranded packaging by default for off-Amazon channels; reported 2026 fee increase of roughly $0.30-$0.41 per unit on 1-2 unit orders; 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge from May 2, 2026; holiday peak fees Oct 15, 2026 to Jan 14, 2027; Preferred Pricing up to 15% off. All from third-party blogs. — [Easyship](https://www.easyship.com/blog/amazon-multi-channel-fulfillment); [Amzprep](https://amzprep.com/amazon-mcf-fees/) (UNVERIFIED)
- Private label minimums: RainShadow Labs states no minimum for buying direct/wholesale, but stock formulas 10 gallons minimum and custom formulas 25 gallons; supplement minimums reported at 500-5,000 units per SKU, some near 100; one source lists a supplement maker at 1,500. Nothing found on tea or socks. — [RainShadow Labs](https://www.rainshadowlabs.com/pages/private-label) (vendor self-report, UNVERIFIED); [Creme de Mint](https://cremedemint.com/blog/industry/herbs-supplements/low-moq-private-label-supplements) (UNVERIFIED)

### Inferences
- The Spocket and Zendrop fees are small relative to product cost, but the real constraint is that they resell catalog items, so the brand cannot control formulation, quality drift or listing removal.
- A "per-order" product (one shipment per month) works through Printful-type POD only for printed goods; consumable subscriptions need a stable SKU and batch consistency, which favors small-batch private label or a stocked 3PL.
- Amazon MCF's unbranded default means it does not satisfy a branded unboxing, and its fees rose in 2026; it is a backup, not the plan.

### Gaps

- No published shipping-cost tables retrieved for any provider.
- No primary source for no-MOQ tea, snack or sock makers.

## 2. Shopify subscription tooling

### Takeaway
Shopify's own Subscriptions app supports skip, pause, cancel and discounts, with fees and processor requirements not stated on the help page I fetched. Third-party apps charge a monthly fee and often 0.75-1.5% plus a per-transaction fee (Recharge, Loop, Skio), while Appstle and Seal are described as having no transaction fee. All numbers below are from vendor blogs and disagree; treat as UNVERIFIED until read from each app's Shopify App Store listing.

### Cited Findings
- Shopify Subscriptions app: weekly/monthly/yearly auto-billing; customers can skip, pause, resume, cancel, update payment and address; multiple plans per product; discount types include percentage and buy X get Y; analytics on active/paused/canceled. Swap and dunning not mentioned on the page; fees and gateway requirement not stated. — [Shopify Help Center](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/purchase-options/shopify-subscriptions) (primary, checked 2026-10-27)
- Third-party fee claims: Recharge $99/mo plus 1.49% + 19 cents; Loop $99/mo plus 0.75% (another source says Loop is custom-priced); Skio $499/mo + 1% + 20 cents (another says $599/mo); Appstle from free, top tier $100/mo, 0% transaction fee; Seal flat, free tier to 50 subscriptions. — [Recurpay](https://recurpay.com/subscription-apps-for-shopify); [Getonecart](https://www.getonecart.com/shopify-subscription-apps/) (vendor-authored, UNVERIFIED)
- Claimed ownership change: several sources say Recharge acquired Skio in April 2026 ($105 million), with no independent confirmation found. — [Ringly](https://www.ringly.io/blog/bold-subscriptions-alternatives) (UNVERIFIED)
- Recharge's and Appstle's own pricing URLs failed (redirect to getrecharge.com not followed; Appstle 404). Not retried.

### Inferences
- At a small scale, a free or flat-fee app (Shopify native, Appstle, Seal) avoids a percentage take; a percent-fee app costs roughly 1-1.5% on top of the 2.9% + $0.30 card fee already in the finance model.

### Gaps
- Dunning/retry, swap, cancellation-flow features and processor requirements for each app: not verified from primary pages. Bold: no data. Age-verification integration with subscriptions: not researched (cost-model file cites $2-$29.99/month app listings, not verified here).

## 3. Subscription box and kitting 3PLs for small brands

### Takeaway
Published pricing is thin. ShipBob and ShipMonk do not publish full rate cards in the material I retrieved, and reported minimums are monthly fees ($250-$275), not unit minimums. I found nothing on whether they accept multi-supplier dropship inbound.

### Cited Findings
- ShipBob: no public price sheet; reported minimum about $275/mo (RevenueGeeks, Toolradar) versus $5-$275 range (Findstack); fit described as 300+ orders/month; setup/integration about 4-8 weeks (Dupple). — [Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/shipbob-vs-shipstation-3pl); [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/shipbob/pricing); [Dupple](https://dupple.com/reviews/shipbob) (all UNVERIFIED third-party)
- ShipMonk: reported $0 setup, $250/mo minimum, $25/pallet storage; another says no minimum order volume. Fulfillrite: from $2.85 per order, 100 orders/month minimum. — [fulfill.com](https://www.fulfill.com/shipbob-vs-shipmonk); [ecommerce-platforms](https://ecommerce-platforms.com/articles/small-business-fulfillment-centers) (UNVERIFIED)

### Inferences
- Under about 100 orders a month, 3PL minimums and onboarding probably cost more than self-fulfilling or using a POD/dropship partner.

### Gaps
- Shipwire, Ships.com-type, Cratejoy fulfillment, local kitting: not researched. Kitting fees, packaging inserts and multi-supplier inbound policies: no retrieved source.

## 4. Legal and platform rules for recurring dropship

### Takeaway
The FTC's 30-day rule applies to internet orders and does not carve out dropshippers on the page I read. Federal click-to-cancel was vacated in July 2025 and rulemaking restarted in March 2026, but ROSCA and state auto-renewal laws (California, New York) still apply. Not legal advice; "For a professional": a lawyer for ARL/ROSCA and an accountant for nexus.

### Cited Findings
- Mail, Internet or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule: sellers must have a reasonable basis to ship within the advertised time, 30 days if none stated; if late, get consent to delay or refund. The page does not address dropshipping. — [FTC rule page](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule) (primary, checked 2026-10-27)
- Eighth Circuit vacated the FTC Click-to-Cancel rule on July 8, 2025 (Custom Communications v. FTC), on procedural grounds. — [Fenwick](https://www.fenwick.com/insights/publications/eighth-circuit-vacates-ftcs-click-to-cancel-rule) (law firm)
- FTC announced a new ANPRM on negative option marketing March 11, 2026 (Federal Register March 13, 2026; comment deadline reported April 13, 2026). ROSCA remains enforceable; the ANPRM notes five litigated actions and six settlements since January 2025. I found nothing on any proposed rule after the comment period. — [Federal Register PDF](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-03-13/pdf/2026-04952.pdf); [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/ftc-restarts-negative-option-rulemaking-after-eighth-circuit-vacatur-enforcement-under-rosca-continues/); [Cooley](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-03-19-ftc-issues-new-advance-notice-of-proposed-rulemaking-on-negative-option-marketing)
- California ARL (Bus. & Prof. Code 17602): online sign-up requires online cancellation (SB 313, effective 2018); AB 390 (effective July 1, 2022) added renewal notices and an easy-to-use online cancel (direct link or button); penalties and UCL class actions possible. — [Mintz](https://mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/2018-06-stricter-automatic-renewal-law-california-commences-july-1-2018); [Manatt](https://manatt.com/insights/newsletters/advertising-law/ca-renews-auto-renewal-law) (law firm; statute page itself 403)
- New York amended ARL (GOL 527/527-a) effective Nov 5, 2025: disclose material terms before collecting billing info, cancel via a mechanism at least as easy as sign-up, price-increase consent or 14-day cancel window. Pending bills S5615-A/A3928-B would require online-only cancel; enactment unconfirmed. NYC has a separate rule requiring an online mechanism. — [Ashurst](https://www.ashurstperkinscoie.com/insights/update/new-york-and-colorado-update-auto-renewing-subscription-requirements); [NY Senate S5615](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S5615)
- Sales tax: post-Wayfair economic nexus; marketplace facilitators collect; for dropship, a supplier without nexus in the destination state generally collects tax unless given a valid resale certificate, and roughly 10-15 states require a certificate tied to their own registration. — [Mondaq](https://admin.mondaq.com/unitedstates/sales-taxes-vat-gst/807858/how-the-wayfair-ruling-weighs-down-drop-shippers-what-you-need-to-know); [PA Dept of Revenue bulletin](https://www.revenue.pa.gov:443/TaxLawPoliciesBulletinsNotices/TaxBulletins/SUT/Documents/st_bulletin_2019-01.pdf) (secondary / state bulletin)
- Shopify Payments: usable only subject to onboarding, underwriting and a prohibited/restricted list; Shopify may impose reserves; the reserves page names extended billing cycles such as annual subscriptions as especially exposed to disputes. Forum reports of 10-20% reserves for 90-180 days on dropshipping stores are anecdotal. — [Shopify Payments terms](https://www.shopify.com/legal/terms-payfac); [Shopify reserves help](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/reserves); [Shopify community thread](https://community.shopify.com/t/20-reserve-on-payouts-because-of-dropshipping/347340/3) (UNVERIFIED)
- Meta: one advertising blog says there is no formal ban on the dropshipping model, with enforcement aimed at misleading claims and slow shipping. — [Agrowth](https://agrowth.io/blogs/facebook-ads/facebook-dropshipping-ban) (UNVERIFIED)

### Inferences
- Dropship lead times of 7-22 days (China origin) sit inside the 30-day default but leave little margin; a stated ship-by time on the product page is needed to avoid the rule's default.
- A monthly-renewing subscription sold online should assume online-only cancellation in CA and NY.

### Gaps
- Not found: primary Meta, TikTok Shop, Amazon, Etsy dropshipping policies; other states' ARLs; whether any state rule exempts or targets dropshipped goods; FTC Business Guide text on dropship responsibility; processor-specific subscription underwriting (Stripe, PayPal).

## 5. Lead times, QC and failure modes

### Takeaway
Evidence retrieved is mostly consistent on one point: variable shipping times and stock-variant mismatch are the recurring complaints, and nothing retrieved gives a primary-source rate of supplier delistings.

### Cited Findings
- Variable shipping, variant mismatch at US warehouses and weak support are the CJ complaint themes. — [Spocket CJ overview](https://www.spocket.co/blogs/cjdropshipping-overview) (UNVERIFIED)
- ShipBob onboarding about 4-8 weeks. — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/reviews/shipbob) (UNVERIFIED)

### Inferences
- Order samples of the exact variant from the US warehouse, and re-sample on a schedule, because drift cannot be seen from a listing. Failure modes to design for: listing removal, price change, slow shipping, unbranded parcel, margin squeeze against 15% subscription discount and processing fees.

### Gaps
- No published data on delisting frequency, price-change frequency or QC drift; no Reddit/Trustpilot threads retrieved.
